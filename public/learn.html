One course for all is obsolete

Nobody learns from the same course.

Your team doesn't share one skill gap — so why do they share one course? Learn starts every employee with a short diagnostic, then builds each one a personal path: skip what they already know, focus where they're weak, practice what matters.

The sequence

How it works.

Upload

Give it your material.

Drop in your source content. Studio reads it and generates the diagnostic, the lessons, and the quiz bank — you review and approve.

Diagnose

Find each person's gaps.

A short check before the course begins. Not a test — a map. Strong topics fold away. Weak ones become the course.

Learn

A path built for one.

Each employee gets their own sequence: focused lessons, quick checks, real interactions. In their language, at their level, with an AI coach one tap away.

Prove

Show the delta.

A final quiz — different questions for every colleague — closes the loop. You don't get completion rates. You get before-and-after, per topic, per person.

The number that matters

Completion is not learning.

Every LMS can tell you who finished. Learn tells you who improved — diagnostic score to final score, topic by topic, rep by rep, rolled up across the whole team. That's the number a training budget is supposed to buy.

Custom, not improvised

Every path is personal. Every word is approved.

The path adapts to each employee — the content never freelances. Every lesson, question, and answer comes from material your trainer uploaded and signed off before the course went live. Personalization decides the order and the focus, never the facts. We do not teach mistakes.

Learning meets practice

Read it. Then live it.

Learn and Practice are one platform. A course can end where it should: in a live simulation with an AI customer, applying exactly what was just learned. Knowledge that gets rehearsed is knowledge that sticks.

Your data stays yours

Same rule as everything we build.

Your training material goes in. Nothing else does. No employee PII beyond a first name, no system connections, no data handover.
