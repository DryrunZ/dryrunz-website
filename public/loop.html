The cycle

How the loop turns.

Target

Choose the metric.

A manager picks the number that matters this quarter. That target shapes everything downstream.

Listen

Feed it real calls.

Upload the transcripts you choose to share. Loop scores every conversation across 50+ dimensions and mines the exact skills that separate your best calls from the rest.

Approve

You stay in charge.

Loop proposes the skills and the training. The manager reviews and approves. Nothing reaches a rep without a human deciding it should.

Train

Weekly, personal, small.

Each rep gets a short weekly step — read it, check it, then rehearse it in a live simulation. Built from their own gaps, aimed at your metric.

Measure

Watch the number.

Next month's calls show whether the line bent. Then the loop turns again.

A new approach

We didn't speed up coaching. We replaced the pipeline.

Everyone else stops at analysis: score the calls, chart the gaps, hand the manager a report. Loop is the first to go the whole way — it mines skills straight from your calls and your goals, then immediately turns each gap into something a rep can practice. From insight to rehearsal in one motion, no workshop in between. That's frontline optimization, not frontline reporting.

Why it works

Coaching that scales past your calendar.

No manager can listen to every call. Loop can — and it turns what it hears into per-rep coaching and per-rep practice, benchmarked against peers. Your best manager's attention, for every rep, every week.

Your data, your choice

Loop is opt-in by design.

Practice and Learn run on zero customer data — always. Loop is the one place real transcripts enter the picture, and only the ones you choose to share, only when you're ready. Your data is optional. Improvement isn't.
