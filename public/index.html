How you win

Every simulation has a way to win.

We don't grade people with a wall of metrics. Each simulation sets a mission a rep can hold in their head — and diamonds mark the wins. Reps retry until they earn them. The retries are where the learning lives.

  • Mission goals.

    Win criteria your team defines, in plain language. What matters to your business is what counts as winning.

  • Skill.

    Communication quality — empathy, clarity, leading the call — judged on the conversation itself, with coaching attached.

  • Efficiency.

    Time and focus. Every minute on a call is money; practice teaches reps to spend it well.

Your data stays yours

Zero data required. Really.

We never ask for your recordings, your CRM, or your knowledge base. Studio builds realistic customers from what your team already knows — the situations, the objections, the policies. Confidential data stays where it belongs: with you.

And when you want the full cycle, Loop, the improvement engine, scores real transcripts you choose to share and turns them into personal practice plans. Your data is optional. Improvement isn't.
