A Great AI Role-Play Is Powered by Three AI Systems — Not One

The conversation may be the most visible part of a simulation. It’s not the most important one.

When people experience an AI role-play for the first time, they naturally focus on the AI actor. Does it sound realistic? Does it interrupt? Is it patient? Difficult? Confused? Does it react naturally?

Those questions matter. Creating believable conversations is what makes practice engaging.

But behind every DryRunZ simulation are two additional AI systems working alongside the actor.

The first is our evaluation engine. As soon as a simulation ends, it analyzes the conversation and produces structured, objective feedback. Every representative receives consistent scoring that can be compared across teams, locations, and time.

The second is our coaching engine — the part where learning actually happens. Instead of simply telling people what they did wrong, it explains why, provides examples, and offers personalized suggestions they can immediately apply in their next conversation.

For many frontline employees, this is the first time anyone has reviewed their customer conversations in a structured, objective, and personalized way. That moment of feedback is where real improvement begins.

The AI conversation may capture your attention. The scoring system measures progress. The coaching system changes behavior. Together, they transform role-play into continuous learning.

Every simulation is powered by three AI systems

  • The AI Actor creates realistic customer conversations.
  • The Evaluation Engine delivers immediate, objective scoring.
  • The Coaching Engine provides personalized, example-based improvement.
  • Together they create measurable learning — not just realistic conversations.
  • Every simulation becomes another coaching opportunity.
