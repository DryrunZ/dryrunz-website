The Best Way to Protect Your Data? Don’t Ask for It.

Most AI platforms begin with data collection. We began by asking a different question: what if we didn’t need it?

Enterprise organizations invest enormous effort protecting customer information, employee records, and proprietary knowledge. They should. Even after vendors complete security reviews, legal approvals, and compliance questionnaires, sharing sensitive data is often the hardest part of deploying new AI systems.

So we built DryRunZ around a different philosophy.

Our AI actors don’t need access to your CRM, customer records, or knowledge base to create realistic conversations. They simply play their role. Behind the scenes, our proprietary evaluation engine measures communication quality, professionalism, and performance without relying on confidential business information.

Whether you’re building simulations in Studio or using Loop to turn sanitized conversation transcripts into personalized training, one principle never changes: your sensitive data stays with you.

This approach doesn’t replace enterprise security — it complements it. Security reviews remain important, and we’re built to meet enterprise expectations. But because we don’t require access to confidential systems or personally identifiable information (PII), deployment is often dramatically simpler.

Instead of spending months integrating with core systems, many organizations can begin improving frontline performance within weeks. That’s not because we cut corners. It’s because we removed one of the biggest barriers before the project even starts.

Our philosophy in practice

  • No CRM or knowledge base connection required
  • No confidential customer information needed for AI role-play
  • Personal information is removed before learning begins in Loop
  • Enterprise-grade security without making sensitive data a dependency
  • Faster deployments, faster adoption, and continuous learning from day one

The less sensitive data an AI platform needs, the easier it becomes to trust, deploy, and scale across the enterprise.
