From Role-Play to Recognition: Dry Runz Takes 2nd Place at FinQuest 2025

A €10,000 award from Alpha Bank validates a simpler way to make enterprise training realistic, measurable, and scalable.

Large sales and support teams need repeated practice, but traditional role-play depends on trainers, schedules, and expensive live coaching. Dry Runz replaces that bottleneck with realistic speech-to-speech AI simulations that let employees practice difficult customer conversations whenever they need to.

The platform was awarded second place and a €10,000 prize at Alpha Bank’s FinQuest 2025. More than an award, it is recognition of a practical idea: effective training should be fast to create, easy to scale, measurable from day one, and possible without connecting to sensitive enterprise systems.

What makes it work

  • Create a new simulation in about five minutes — no prompting expertise required
  • Give supervisors transcripts, recordings, structured feedback, and progress data
  • Train with synthetic scenarios without CRM, database, or backend access
  • Scale across large teams with speech-to-speech and text-based practice
  • Pay according to actual usage, without per-seat or upfront capacity costs
