Great Simulations Don’t Happen by Accident

Behind every realistic role-play is a platform designed to make complexity feel simple.

Creating enterprise-grade simulations isn’t about writing one good prompt. It’s about giving learning teams the right building blocks while hiding the technical complexity underneath.

That’s why DryRunZ combines dozens of capabilities into a workflow that anyone can use. Start with the basics, launch your first simulation, then gradually add more advanced features as your confidence grows.

Need a specific customer persona? Choose one from the actor catalog or create your own.

Want AI assistance? Let it help generate scenarios, prompts, and simulation content.

Need realistic working conditions? Add background noise, timers, CRM screens, synthetic customer records, PDFs, or internal procedures — without connecting to live enterprise systems.

Need organization-specific coaching? Define your own evaluation criteria and coaching DNA so every recommendation reflects how your company wants conversations to happen.

Power is important. Simplicity is essential. Our goal has always been to give instructional designers enterprise-level flexibility without requiring them to become prompt engineers.

Built for real-world training

  • Ready-made or custom AI actors
  • AI-assisted simulation creation
  • Background sounds, timers, and realistic environments
  • Synthetic CRM data, PDFs, and mock customer information
  • Organization-specific coaching and evaluation criteria
  • Automatic coaching recommendations based on performance
  • A simple interface that grows with your needs
