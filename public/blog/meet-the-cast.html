Meet the Cast: Great Training Starts with the Right AI Actor

Every conversation is different. Your AI actors should be too.

Choosing the right actor is one of the most important parts of building an effective simulation. Sometimes you need the angry customer. Other times, the confused one, the gossip, the impatient executive, or the friendly person who simply talks too much.

Think back to theater class: even a great script will not work with the wrong casting. The scenario defines what happens, but the actor determines how the conversation feels — and what skills the rep has to practice.

DryRunZ gives you a ready-made catalog of battle-tested AI actors. Each actor has a distinct communication style, personality, and background, helping you build simulations that feel realistic and meaningfully different from one another.

And when the perfect actor is not in the catalog, simply create one. Answer three short questions, and DryRunZ generates a private AI actor built specifically for your simulation.

  • Choose from a catalog of battle-tested AI actors
  • Train against different personalities and communication styles
  • Match the right actor to each learning objective
  • Create a private actor by answering three simple questions
  • Turn every scenario into a more realistic conversation
