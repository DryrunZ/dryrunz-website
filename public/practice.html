Describe the situation, the customer, and what winning means. Studio — our authoring engine — turns it into a live simulation in under five minutes. No prompting skills. No content team. No integration project.

How you win

Every simulation has a way to win.

We don't grade people with a wall of metrics. Each simulation sets a mission a rep can hold in their head — and diamonds mark the wins. Reps retry until they earn them. The retries are where the learning lives.

Mission goals.

Win criteria your team defines, in plain language. What matters to your business is what counts as winning.

Skill.

Communication quality — empathy, clarity, leading the call — judged on the conversation itself, with coaching attached.

Efficiency.

Time and focus. Every minute on a call is money; practice teaches reps to spend it well.

After every run

A coach, not a scoreboard.

Every run ends with a short spoken debrief that points at the exact moment — and the line to try next time. Then the retry is right there. Managers see the trend report; nobody reads transcripts.

The math is short

Shorter training pays for the platform.

Practice compresses training. Onboarding weeks become days; workshop hours become simulation minutes — and every hour a rep isn't in a classroom is an hour on the floor. Cut training time by a fraction, and DryRunZ has already paid for itself. Everything after that is upside.

Your data stays yours

Runz on zero customer data.

We never ask for your recordings, your CRM, or your knowledge base. Practice runs entirely on synthetic customers built from what your team already knows. Confidential data stays where it belongs: with you.
